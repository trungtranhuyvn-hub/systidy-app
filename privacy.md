# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

SysTidy does not collect any data. Everything it measures stays on your Mac.

## What the app reads

To show what takes up space, SysTidy reads file and folder metadata on your Mac: names, sizes and modification dates. It does not open or read the contents of your files.

## What the app stores

Your settings (language, icon, menu bar option) and the permission to read the Home folder you chose. They are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's own container.

## Who can see it

Only you. SysTidy has no server, no account and no network code. Scan results are never sent anywhere.

## No tracking, analytics or sharing

SysTidy has no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Nothing is sold or shared.

## Deleting files

SysTidy only removes files you select and confirm. By default they are moved to the Trash.

## Deleting your data

Removing SysTidy from your Mac removes its settings.

## Children

SysTidy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
